# Dino Bones

## Where is the options page?

The [player options page for this game](../player-options) contains all the options you need to configure and
export a config file.

## What does randomization do to this game?

Each of the nine skeletons is locked behind its own **Exhibit Permit**. Without a species' permit you cannot place
bones on that skeleton. You start with one random permit; the other eight are items. The **Polaroid Camera**, the
**Watering Can**, the **Glider** and the **Crate Hammer** (crate breaking) are items too, and finding their secret in the museum
is a check instead of granting the tool. The rest of the pool is **Museum Donations** (a toast, nothing more).

## What is a check?

- Completing each skeleton, and revealing each species' secret (always).
- Placing bones on a skeleton: every 10% or every single bone (`bonesanity`).
- Placing each species' coin, cassette and book (`collectionsanity`).
- Photographing the exhibits, the cat, and the model with the snow globe (`photosanity`).
- Maxing out the garden beds (`gardensanity`).
- Finding the model kit pieces (`kitsanity`).
- Straightening skeleton supports (`supportsanity`).
- Unlocking the 29 achievements (`achievementsanity`).

## What is the goal?

Complete all nine skeletons and watch the ending (`endgame`), or complete `skeletons_needed` skeletons.

## What happens when I receive an item?

The client hands it to the game plugin right away: permits unlock bone placement for that species and equipment
appears in your inventory.

## Notes

Archipelago runs use their own save slot, so your normal Dino Bones save is never touched.
